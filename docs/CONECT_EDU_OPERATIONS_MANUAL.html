CONECT EDU Operations Manual

This manual describes how each area of the CONECT EDU platform operates for parents, schools, and platform administrators.

1. Platform Roles

  • Parent: creates an account, explores schools, submits applications for a child, pays fees, uploads supporting documents, and receives updates.
  • School administrator: manages one approved school profile, its branches, applications, admission decisions, and staff access.
  • School staff: signs in to the school portal with the access level issued by a school manager.
  • Platform administrator: monitors the platform, approves schools, manages users and payments, exports reports, and reviews activity.

2. Parent Registration and Sign-in

  1. A parent registers with their name, email address, phone number, and password.
  2. The platform creates a parent account. Student accounts are not used; parents act on behalf of their children.
  3. Parents sign in through the main login page and are taken to their dashboard.
  4. The Forgot Password page accepts an email address and confirms the reset request. Email or OTP delivery is ready to be connected later to a messaging provider.

3. Discovering and Comparing Schools

  1. Parents use the Schools page to browse listed institutions and narrow results by available filters.
  2. Each school page displays its profile, academics, admissions instructions, requirements, documents, facilities, gallery, and contact information.
  3. The Compare page allows a parent to compare two schools side by side.
  4. Parents may save schools in their browser for quick access later.

4. School Branches and Capacity

  1. A school is listed once, even where it operates several campuses.
  2. In School Profile, a school administrator adds each branch on its own line using: branch name, street address, city, state, capacity, application deadline, and application fee.
  3. Branches appear on the public school page, and the parent chooses a preferred branch during the application.
  4. The platform blocks applications to a branch after its deadline or after its capacity has been reached.
  5. The selected branch is saved with the application and displayed to the admissions team.

5. Parent Application Process

  1. The parent opens a school page and selects Apply Now.
  2. The application collects child details, current school/class, preferred class, and parent contact details.
  3. For multi-branch schools, the parent must select a branch.
  4. School-created questions are displayed and required answers are checked by both the browser and the server.
  5. The parent reviews the application, then proceeds to payment where applicable.
  6. After payment is successful, the parent submits the application to the school.

6. Application Documents and Status

  1. On an application details page, parents can upload supported documents such as birth certificates, passport photographs, school reports, transfer documents, and other files.
  2. Documents are stored with the individual application and are only available to the parent, the relevant school, and authorized platform administrators.
  3. Parents can view application status, assessment details, decision notes, branch selection, and payment information.
  4. When a school changes an application status, the parent receives an in-app update on the parent dashboard.

7. School Onboarding and Login

  1. A prospective school submits its onboarding form with contact and location information.
  2. A platform administrator reviews the request and either approves or rejects it.
  3. Approval creates the school listing and its initial school administrator account.
  4. Approved schools use the School Administrator Login page, not the parent login page.

8. School Profile and Admissions Controls

  1. School administrators maintain their public description, contact details, curricula, facilities, programmes, admission instructions, and admission requirements.
  2. Schools may add required custom questions for all parent applications.
  3. Schools may upload a logo, cover image, gallery images, and admission documents for parents to download.
  4. Each update is recorded in the platform activity log.

9. School Applications and Staff

  1. School users see only applications submitted to their own school.
  2. The Applications page supports search plus status and branch filters.
  3. Admissions staff can set a decision, an assessment date where relevant, and an optional note for the parent.
  4. School managers may create staff accounts. Admissions officers handle admissions work, finance officers have finance-oriented access, and school managers have full school management access.
  5. New submitted applications create in-app notifications for school administrators.

10. Platform Administration

  1. All platform administrators can monitor schools, users, applications, payments, and reports.
  2. The configured owner administrator can create other platform administrators and grant them permission to manage administrator accounts.
  3. Platform administrators approve school onboarding requests and monitor existing schools.
  4. Reports can be exported as CSV files for users, schools, applications, and payments.
  5. The Activity Log records key changes, including application status changes, school profile updates, staff creation, and application submission.

11. Payments

  1. An application calculates the applicable application fee and processing fee.
  2. Branch-specific application fees override the standard fee when configured.
  3. The parent must have a successful payment before an application can be submitted to the school.
  4. Platform administrators can review payment records, while school access remains limited to that school.

12. Data, Privacy, and Security

  1. The Privacy Notice and Terms of Use are available from the public site.
  2. Passwords are not displayed in the interface. New accounts require passwords of at least 12 characters.
  3. Access checks limit records to the parent, the applicable school, or authorized platform administrators.
  4. For a production launch, connect managed cloud storage for documents, a managed database, automated backups, and an email/SMS provider for password resets and notifications.

Operational note: The application is ready for demonstration use. Production use should include external backups, secure cloud file storage, and configured email/SMS delivery.
